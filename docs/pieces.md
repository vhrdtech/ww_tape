# Pieces and chains

After this chapter you can cut a long recording into pieces by size or age. You can keep them in a directory or one
file, and delete old ones.

The book does not have this chapter yet. Until it does, [Sessions, pieces and chains](sessions_and_pieces.md), [Pieces
and files](pieces_and_files.md), [Syncing, cuts and relays](syncing_and_cuts.md) and [The definitions a new piece
carries](carried_definitions.md) cover its material.
