# ww_tape changelog

From October 2026, `ww_logbook` and then ww_tape grew inside the wire_weaver repo. In November 2026 it moved to its
own repo ([vhrdtech/ww_tape](https://github.com/vhrdtech/ww_tape)) with its history, fuzz targets and seeds,
benchmarks, golden files and book pages.

## [Unreleased]

### Features

- New crate `ww_tape_mux`: `TapeMux` writes a chain from its own thread; producers send through handles over a byte-bounded queue (WRITE-12).
- `ww_tape_fs::mux::create` and `resume` open a mux on a directory of piece files (WRITE-12).
- `PieceChain::relay_block` and `adopt_block` pass a producer's blocks into a chain; whole chunk blocks cut by policy too (WRITE-12).
- `PieceChain::cut_policy` gives the chain's cut policy (WRITE-12).

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-21

### Breaking

- Track and clock numbers are `TrackNum` and `ClockNum`, 1 and up, everywhere; build them with `TrackNum::new(n)` (READ-5).
- `Record::track`, `RecordRef::track` and `Block::first_track` are a `RecordTrack`; `DESCRIBE_TRACK` and `NO_CLOCK` are gone, use `RecordTrack::Definitions` (READ-5).
- `PushError::DescribeTrack` and `BlockStartError::NoClock` are gone: the types exclude them; `DescribeError::RecordTime` refuses a mismatched `RecordRef` (READ-5).
- `IndexEntry::track_number` is `number`, the raw field; `IndexEntry::track()` gives a chunk's or summary's `TrackNum` (READ-5).
- Track keys, track ids and clock keys are `TrackKey`, `TrackId` and `ClockKey`; wrap literals with `::new`, unwrap with `.get()` (READ-5).
- `StoredRelations` and `TrackFacts` hold `stored` and `monotone` as a `RelationSet`; build one with `RelationSet::from_bits` (READ-5).
- Byte sizes in `NearPolicy`, `IndexPolicy`, `CutPolicy`, `OpenOptions` and summary block caps are `Bytes<T>`, counts `Count<_, u64>` (READ-5).
- Item numbers (`first_item`, `next_item`, `end_item`, timeline and range-hash items) are `ItemNum`; counts stay `u64` (READ-5).
- Define tracks and clocks with `Definitions::define_track(TrackSpec)` and `define_clock(ClockSpec)`, which return their numbers (WRITE-11).
- `Definitions::define` and `define_all` refuse a track or clock: `DescribeError::Numbered` (WRITE-11).
- After a resume, start the definitions with `Definitions::with_numbers(resumed.numbers)` to keep each track's number (WRITE-11).
- `ww_tape_example` drops its number constants: `definitions()` returns the numbers as `DriveNumbers` (WRITE-11).

### Features

- `DefinitionsAt::track_number` finds a track's number by its id among the definitions in force (WRITE-11).
